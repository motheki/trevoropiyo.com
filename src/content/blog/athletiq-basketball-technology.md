AthletIQ is a mobile app for basketball training and player development. The product brings structured practice, progress tracking, and performance feedback into an experience athletes can use during real training sessions.

Trevor Opiyo LLC's work with AthletIQ sits at the intersection of mobile product engineering and applied sports technology.

## Product Scope

AthletIQ is built around basketball workflows that need to feel fast, structured, and useful during training:

- Training experiences that help athletes stay focused during practice.
- Progress views that make performance trends easier to understand.
- Player-facing feedback that turns session activity into useful takeaways.
- Mobile workflows designed for quick use before, during, and after workouts.

## Engineering Focus

The engineering work emphasizes product quality, mobile reliability, and clear feedback loops. Key areas include:

- Building athlete-facing screens that are easy to use in a training context.
- Organizing product flows around practice, progress, and review.
- Translating raw activity into clear, useful performance summaries.
- Improving the app experience through iterative testing and implementation.

## Business Value

AthletIQ needs product surfaces that are useful to athletes while staying reliable in live training environments. The work shows Trevor Opiyo LLC's ability to connect user-facing mobile design with practical sports technology execution.

That range is the point: sports technology products succeed when the app experience, performance feedback, and operating workflow all support the same athlete outcome.
